Game Rules

Goal
Reach the final scene and roll 20+ on a d20 to defeat the boss.

Gameplay
All players control one shared character. Each scene, one player decides which of 3 choices to make. Discuss as a group first.

Choice Outcomes
Damage — Lose 1 HP (0 HP = defeat)
Item — Gain an item (+1 to final roll)
Level Up — Gain a level (+1 roll attempt)

Final Boss
Roll a d20, add your item count as a bonus. Your level = number of roll attempts. Need 20+ total to win.
